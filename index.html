What this view says:
Just over half of the specified surface area is rated, but only a third of SKUs and a quarter of vendors are: the rated vendors supply the big-area materials (textiles, wallcovering, carpet, bedding), while many smaller decorative and lighting lines are unrated.
Coverage rests on a few vendors: the top 5 = 71% of rated surface area. Momentum Textiles & Wallcovering and P/Kaufmann Contract alone are 43%, and Justin David Textiles' blackout fabric adds another 14%.
This is a specification lookback, so the report measures surface area and product counts; there is no purchase-order spend.

What the sustainability icons mean
Score = the sum, across every product in the space, of (product surface area ÷ the space's total surface area) × the product's overall score; unrated products score 0. This reproduces Mindclick's scores (71 overall, 78 Guestrooms, 63 Public Space). SKUs that share the same vendor options are grouped into one item, because the recommendation export gives surface area per vendor product family rather than per SKU; where an option spans product families, its score is area-weighted. Opportunities

The biggest opportunities to raise coverage

Which unrated products, if rated, would move the needle most?

The prize: ~165,500 sq ft (47% of specified surface area) sits with 137 unrated product lines (381 SKUs) from 91 named vendors plus a block of items with no vendor named. Rating the top 5 named vendors by unrated area takes rated surface area from 53% to 64%; Burch Fabrics' seating fabrics and vinyls alone add 3.6 points. A further 19% of area is specified without a manufacturer; Mindclick has recommended rated vendors for most of it (see Recommendations).
